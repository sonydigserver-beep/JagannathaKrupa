About Jagannath Ashtakam

The Jagannath Ashtakam is a revered hymn of eight verses that celebrates Lord Jagannath of Puri. Each verse glorifies the divine form, compassion, and grace of the Lord, culminating in a prayer for eternal remembrance and liberation.

Lord Jagannath, the Lord of the Universe, is worshipped at Puri alongside Balabhadra and Subhadra. The Ashtakam is a heartfelt prayer to behold his divine form and grace.
