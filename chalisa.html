Practice guide

How to Recite

1. Set your intention
Begin with a short prayer and calm breathing.
2. Read with focus
Keep rhythm steady and pronounce each verse clearly.
3. Pause between sections
Take a brief moment after each doha or chaupai.
4. Close with gratitude
Offer thanks and a moment of silence.
