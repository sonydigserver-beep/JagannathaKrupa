Daily Rituals Schedule

The divine daily schedule (Niti) of Lord Jagannath, Lord Balabhadra, and Devi Subhadra

5:00 AM

Mangala Alati (ମଙ୍ଗଳା ଆଳତି)

The first ritual of the day begins with the opening of the temple doors. The deities are awakened with the melodious sounds of bells, conch shells, and cymbals. This auspicious ritual marks the beginning of a new day in the divine presence.

Duration: 30 mins Morning Ritual
6:00 AM

Mailama (ମଇଲମ)

After Mangala Alati, the deities are cleaned and dressed in fresh clothes. This ritual involves washing the deities with sacred water and adorning them with beautiful garments and ornaments.

Duration: 45 mins Purification
8:00 AM

Abakasha (ଅବକାଶ)

The first offering of food (bhoga) is made to the deities. This includes a variety of traditional Odia delicacies prepared in the temple kitchen. The deities are offered breakfast consisting of fruits, sweets, and other sacred foods.

Duration: 1 hour Food Offering
12:00 PM

Madhyanha Dhupa (ମଧ୍ୟାହ୍ନ ଧୂପ)

The midday food offering is one of the most elaborate rituals. A grand feast of 56 dishes (Chhappan Bhoga) is offered to Lord Jagannath. This includes rice, dal, various vegetable preparations, sweets, and savories.

Duration: 1.5 hours 56 Dishes
6:00 PM

Sandhya Dhupa (ସନ୍ଧ୍ୟା ଧୂପ)

The evening food offering ritual begins as the sun sets. Light snacks and sweets are offered to the deities. The temple is illuminated with lamps, creating a divine atmosphere.

Duration: 45 mins Evening Ritual
7:30 PM

Bada Singara Besha (ବଡ ସିଙ୍ଗାର ବେଶ)

The deities are adorned with elaborate decorations and dressed in magnificent attire. This is the grand evening darshan when devotees can have a glimpse of the beautifully decorated deities.

Duration: 1 hour Grand Decoration
10:00 PM

Khata Seja (ଖଟ ଶେଯା)

The final ritual of the day involves preparing the deities for rest. The temple doors are closed after offering prayers and lullabies are sung to help the deities retire for the night.

Duration: 30 mins Night Ritual
