The Sacred Tradition of Bana

The Bana ceremony is one of the highest honors in the Jagannath tradition. Your flag will be hoisted atop the sacred Nila Chakra, where it will flutter in devotion to Lord Jagannath. This ancient ritual symbolizes the surrender of one's ego and the offering of one's prayers to the divine.
